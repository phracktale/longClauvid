## Lettre aux auteurs

Objet : Your ME/CFS Mendelian randomization preprint: the mtDNA copy number estimate

```text
Dear authors,

I maintain longClauvid, an open, source-traceable registry of hypotheses on long COVID and ME/CFS (https://github.com/phracktale/longClauvid). I am an independent software developer, not a clinician or a researcher, and part of the registry was drafted with an AI assistant; every claim in it links to its source.

Your preprint reports that genetically higher mitochondrial DNA copy number is associated with lower ME/CFS risk (OR about 0.83 per SD, primary IVW analysis). The registry lists this as a lead to replicate. The text gives the point estimate, and the Zenodo record that holds the full results is restricted.

My question: could you share, or open, for this exposure: the 95% confidence interval and p value of the IVW estimate; the estimates of the sensitivity analyses shown in your Figure 2 (weighted median and MR-Egger intercept with its interval, if you computed them); the leave-one-out results; and the list of the 84 instruments with their effect estimates?

By my calculation, no public and independent ME/CFS case set is large enough to replicate this estimate today: power ranges from 5% to 18%. The calculation and the analysis plan are public (in French): https://github.com/phracktale/longClauvid/blob/main/analyses/P17/PLAN.md. If you know of a second large ME/CFS GWAS, a pointer would help.

Thank you for your work on this illness.

[name]
```
